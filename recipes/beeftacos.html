Beef Tacos

Home

Description

Beef tacos are a popular Mexican-inspired dish made with seasoned ground beef, crispy or soft taco shells, fresh vegetables, and cheese.

They are quick to prepare and can be customized with your favorite toppings for an easy and delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Taco shells
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Sour cream
  • Taco seasoning
  • Salt and pepper
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add the taco seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cook the beef for a few more minutes.
  5. Chop the lettuce, tomato, and onion.
  6. Warm the taco shells according to the package instructions.
  7. Fill each taco shell with the cooked beef.
  8. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and cheddar cheese.
  9. Add sour cream and serve.
