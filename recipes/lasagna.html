"Meat Cheesy Lasagna"

Home

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned meat, and creamy cheese. It is baked until the top is golden and bubbling.

This comforting recipe is perfect for a family dinner and can be prepared ahead of time.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic until browned.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and Italian seasoning, then simmer.
  4. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat the layers and finish with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Bake until the cheese is golden and bubbling.
  7. Let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving.
