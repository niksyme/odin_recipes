Chicken Alfredo

Home

Description

Chicken Alfredo is a creamy pasta dish made with tender chicken, fettuccine pasta, Parmesan cheese, and a rich Alfredo sauce.

This delicious and comforting recipe is easy to prepare and is perfect for a quick family dinner.

Ingredients

  • Fettuccine pasta
  • Chicken breast
  • Heavy cream
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook the fettuccine according to the package instructions.
  2. Cut the chicken breast into small pieces.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden.
  4. Add the garlic and cook for one minute.
  5. Add the butter and heavy cream to the pan.
  6. Stir in the Parmesan cheese until the sauce becomes creamy.
  7. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything together.
  8. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  9. Serve immediately.
